Engineering landman title systems around the source record.

Landman title research is an unforgiving test of document automation. An extracted grantor name, mineral fraction, or recording reference is useful only if the examining landman can trace it directly to the recorded courthouse instrument and the exact tract under examination.

Exact Domain Arithmetic BigInt Rational Fractions (exact.mjs)

Reduced numerator/denominator representation eliminates IEEE-754 rounding drift across multi-generation conveyances.

Keep the assignment and tract explicit

A title workflow begins with an explicit contract envelope: state, county, survey/abstract or section-township-range legal description, target tract, research period, and required client deliverable. Those identifiers stay bound to records throughout intake, ClamAV scanning, examination, and report assembly. A matching surname or nearby legal description is never sufficient to merge two records into one ownership chain.

Document identity and tract identity are distinct relational axes. A single recorded deed or probate packet can convey interests across several tracts; multiple courthouse files can hold certified or marginal copies of the same instrument. Our data model preserves those many-to-many edges instead of treating every file upload as an isolated conveyance.

Make provenance part of the data model

A source-linked runsheet requires more than a string note in a spreadsheet column. Every extracted observation binds the SHA-256 content digest, county book/volume/page or clerk instrument number, 200-DPI page raster region, and extraction rule revision. In TitleDesk Agent, local Tesseract and @napi-rs/canvas main-process OCR run offline at zero per-page cost while keeping the original courthouse scan one click away.

Model proposals, accepted facts, and unresolved title exceptions occupy separate epistemic states. In Title-Desk, our cross-family 3-model Triple-Check pairs dual blind Gemini 4 Carbon examiners with an independent GPT-6 Astra validator—and still requires explicit human acceptance before an uncertain grantor link advances.

Compute ownership with exact rational arithmetic

Ownership fractions, Duhig-rule reductions, and net mineral acres must be calculated deterministically from explicit rational operands. Our exact.mjs engine represents every fractional interest as a reduced BigInt numerator/denominator pair all the way through aggregation, deferring decimal formatting strictly to presentation columns.

Even exact arithmetic cannot rescue an unsupported premise: a column total of 1/1 does not prove the right heirs or reservations were captured. Reconciliation checks both conservation invariants and primary-source instrument coverage.

Verify the workbook that will actually be delivered

The exported XLSX/PDF client package is verified as an independent artifact. Before release or two-step manual-capture Stripe settlement, an out-of-band reader re-parses the generated workbook to confirm owner names, rational fractions, net mineral acres, recording citations, and unresolved-issue schedules match the accepted working ledger byte-for-byte.

Unresolved title defects retain an explicit machine-readable disposition; qualified landmen and reviewing title attorneys retain final authority over legal title conclusions.
