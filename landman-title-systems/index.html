Engineering landman title systems around the source record.

Landman title research is an unforgiving test of document automation. An extracted grantor name, mineral fraction, or recording reference is useful only if the examining landman can trace it directly to the recorded courthouse instrument and the exact tract under examination.

Verification & Scope Provenance · Production architecture reference for live Texas mineral-interest platforms (title-desk.com, desktop.title-desk.com, artificiallandman.io); repository test counts pinned at commit 8a2cc971 as of 2026-11-18.

Keep the assignment and tract explicit

A title workflow begins with an explicit contract envelope scoped to Texas, one county per run, and current mineral interest owners only (interestType: ['MI']): county, survey/abstract legal description, target tract, research period, and draft deliverable scope. Those identifiers stay bound to records throughout intake, ClamAV malware scanning on the Linux hosted worker, examination, and workbook assembly. A matching surname or nearby legal description is never sufficient to merge two records into one ownership chain.

Document identity and tract identity are distinct relational axes. A single recorded deed or probate packet can convey mineral interests across several tracts; multiple courthouse files can hold certified or marginal copies of the same instrument. Our data model preserves those many-to-many edges instead of treating every file upload as an isolated conveyance.

Make provenance part of the data model

A source-linked runsheet requires more than a string note in a spreadsheet column. Every extracted observation binds the SHA-256 content digest, county book/volume/page or clerk instrument number, 200-DPI page raster region, and extraction rule revision. In TitleDesk Agent (the TitleDesk desktop app across Windows, macOS, and Linux), zero-cost local Tesseract and @napi-rs/canvas main-process OCR run offline while keeping the original courthouse scan one click away.

Model proposals, verified ownership rows, and unresolved curative exceptions occupy separate epistemic states. In TitleDesk and Artificial Landman (deployed 2026-11-18 at commits 2ac23b2 and f9e8dcf), two Gemini sessions examine the records separately while a deterministic program checks every fraction. A third model, from a different provider, then checks the final answer against the page images: if it finds a problem, the examiner gets one repair and the third model checks the repaired answer again. Anything still unresolved stays a proposal—never a verified ownership—and its finding is listed first among the curative requirements; if the third model cannot run, the workbook says so.

Compute ownership with exact rational arithmetic

Current mineral ownership fractions, Duhig-rule reductions, and net mineral acres must be calculated deterministically from explicit rational operands. Our exact.mjs engine represents every fractional mineral interest as a reduced BigInt numerator/denominator pair all the way through aggregation, deferring decimal formatting strictly to presentation columns.

Even exact arithmetic cannot rescue an unsupported premise: a column total of 1/1 does not prove the right heirs or reservations were captured. Reconciliation checks both conservation invariants and primary-source instrument coverage.

Verify the workbook that will actually be delivered

Hosted runs on title-desk.com and artificiallandman.io are prepaid before the run starts (a run finishing with unresolved items or missing records remains charged, while a failed run returns a run credit rather than a cash refund; invitation-only Hybrid and Full service runs bound county record-retrieval copy budgets with explicit reservation holds). From hosted commits 1fcd538 (titledesk-hosted) and 9c8af3e (artificiallandman-hosted), deployed 2026-11-18, after the four-sheet .xlsx workbook is written, a separate reader reopens the file and compares every owner, mineral interest fraction, net mineral acres figure, and curative requirement against the examination JSON—stopping delivery and handling the run as failed on any mismatch, splitting cells over Excel's 32,767-character limit rather than silently cutting them, and writing fractions whose numerator or denominator has more than 15 digits as exact text rather than a rounded number.

Every delivered workbook is a draft for review by a qualified landman or attorney—never a title opinion and never a certification. Unresolved title defects remain explicit proposals leading the curative requirements so examining landmen and reviewing title attorneys keep final authority over legal conclusions.

Live platforms & commercial integration

Explore TitleDesk (title-desk.com) for independent landmen and abstractors, TitleDesk Agent (desktop.title-desk.com) across Windows, macOS, and Linux, and Artificial Landman (artificiallandman.io), which runs the same Texas mineral-interest engine as TitleDesk packaged for land teams, brokerages and operators. Upload examination runs are open to everyone while Hybrid and Full county-record retrieval runs are by invitation only.
